# Scope and Risk Assessment
## Scope

The scope of this audit covers Botium Toys' security environment, including employee devices, the internal network, business systems, customer and payment data, e-commerce services, data storage, legacy systems, and physical security controls.
## Audit Goals

The goals of this audit are to:

- Review the organization's existing assets and security controls
- Identify security gaps and weaknesses
- Assess risks to critical assets and sensitive information
- Review compliance practices
- Recommend security improvements to reduce risk and strengthen Botium Toys' security posture
